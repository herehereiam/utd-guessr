How to play
  1. You start with a cropped image.
  2. Click "Reveal more" up to three times to see more of the image.
  3. Each reveal subtracts 1,000 points from your maximum score, which starts at 5,000.
  4. Click the map to guess where on campus the crosshair in the image is. You only get one guess.
  5. Guesses within 75 feet of the true location earn maximum points. Guesses that are off by more than 600 feet earn less than half of the maximum points.
